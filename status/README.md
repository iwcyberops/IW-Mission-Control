### 📋 Status Modules:

1. ⚡ **[`daily-progress-log.md`](./daily-progress-log.md):**  
   The active, living ledger of the current operational month. Records granular daily execution across all 7 cognitive blocks, daily goals, encountered roadblocks, and technical fixes.

2. 🎯 **[`milestones.md`](./milestones.md):**  
   The executive checklist and portfolio verification board. Tracks completion of the **27 Master Portfolio Projects**, 5 Phase Capstone Defenses, and formal credential acquisitions.

3. 🗄️ **[`monthly-archive/`](./monthly-archive/):**  
   The immutable historical vault. When a 30-day month concludes, its daily records are compiled and preserved here as standalone monthly dossiers (`month-01-progress.md`, `month-02-progress.md`, etc.).
